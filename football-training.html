⚽ Football Training Guide

Improve your football skills with structured training.

🎯 Shooting Training

  • Practice finishing from different angles.
  • Work on both-foot finishing.
  • Practice controlled shots and accuracy.

⚡ Dribbling Training

  • Practice close ball control.
  • Use cone-based dribbling drills.
  • Practice changing direction quickly.

🎯 Passing Training

  • Practice short and long passing.
  • Work on your weaker foot.
  • Practice passing while moving.

🏃 Speed & Fitness

  • Short acceleration drills.
  • Agility and direction-change drills.
  • Build football-specific endurance gradually.

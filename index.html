🗺️ Master Training Schedules & Elite Roadmap

By Skill Tiers & Age Groups
🌱 Beginner Tier (Ages 8-12): Foundation Building
• Time Commitment: 45 mins/day, 3 days a week.
• Focus: Ball touch, basic passing against a wall, coordination, and having fun with the ball.
⚡ Professional Tier (Ages 13-17): Tactical & Physical Integration
• Time Commitment: 90 mins/day, 5 days a week + weekend match.
• Focus: First touch under pressure, position-specific runs, stamina, and basic bodyweight strength.
🔥 Elite Academy Tier (Ages 18+): Professional Standard
• Time Commitment: 2 hours on-pitch + gym session daily.
• Focus: High-intensity tactical execution, explosive sprints, and elite recovery tracking.
🏠 What to do AT HOME:
• Ball Mastery: 1,000 touches daily using sole rolls, toe taps, and inside-out chops in a tight room.
• Wall Passing: 100 accurate passes with weak foot and strong foot against a wall.
⚽ What to do AT THE GROUND:
• Dribbling at Speed: Cone weaving and driving past defenders at full acceleration.
• Long Passing & Crossing: Switching play and curling crosses into the box.
⚡ Before Training (Dynamic Warm-Up):
• 5 mins light jogging & skipping rope.
• Dynamic stretches: Leg swings, high knees, opening the gate, and lunges.
🧊 After Training (Cool Down & Recovery):
• 5 mins slow walk and static stretching (hamstrings, calves).
• Immediate rehydration with water and electrolytes.
🛡️ How to Avoid Common Football Injuries (ACL, Ankles, Hamstrings):
• Ankle Stability: Incorporate balance board or single-leg standing exercises.
• Hamstring Strength: Perform Nordic curls or Romanian deadlifts to bulletproof sprinting muscles.
